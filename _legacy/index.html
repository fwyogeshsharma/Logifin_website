How fast is funding, really? +

For first-time invoices on the platform, 24–48 hours from upload to disbursal. Once your business has a verified history, 70% of repeat invoices fund within 4 hours.

What does it cost? +

Rates start at 1.1% per month and depend on shipper rating, route, and tenor. No setup fees, no minimums, no penalty for early settlement.

Who actually lends the money? +

A network of regulated NBFCs, family offices, and qualified institutional investors. LogiFin is the technology and risk layer; capital comes from licensed partners under RBI-compliant structures.

What happens if a shipper doesn't pay? +

We pursue collection through our dedicated recoveries team. Pool-level loss provisioning across lenders means individual exposure stays low. Net default rate on the platform is currently 0.41%.

Do I need to be in a specific state? +

Active in 14 states across India's major freight corridors: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Gujarat, Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, and Kerala.
